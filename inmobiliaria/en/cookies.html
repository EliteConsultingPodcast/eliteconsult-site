Cookies

An outline of a cookie policy. It describes today’s draft and leaves gaps for the day when real cookies exist.

Draft with no legal effect

Draft outline, November 2026. No legal review.

1. What this template does

  • The code of these pages does not create its own cookies.
  • There is no analytics, no advertising and no social button that would set a cookie.
  • Language is changed with a link. No preference is stored in the browser.
  • The typefaces live on the site itself. A visit does not ask Google for the letters.

That does not close the question. Before publication, a lawyer or a gestoría should look at whether the host adds any cookie or log that has to be declared. [Host – not yet chosen].

2. Gaps for later

If cookies are added one day, the policy will have to separate them and state the name, the purpose, who sets them and how long they last. Today every box is empty:

  • Strictly necessary: [not used in this draft].
  • Preferences: [not used in this draft].
  • Measurement: [not used in this draft].
  • Marketing: [not used in this draft].

3. Consent

Gap. Before a cookie that is not necessary is set, the proper basis will be needed and, where the rule requires it, prior consent. The mechanism does not exist yet: [Consent tool – pending]. There is no banner in this draft, and it should not be pretended that there is one.

4. The browser

A visitor can delete or block cookies in the browser settings. The exact steps depend on the program they use. This page does not link to third-party instructions, because the draft, by its own code, does not set cookies.
