Services

Services

Three possible instructions. In all three the manner is the same: a written proposal, one person to speak to, and no result promised in advance.

Selling a home

For someone who wants to bring a home to market, in the neighbourhoods named on the home page.

  • A conversation about the asking price, based on what you know of the property and on what can be seen in public listings. It is not a formal valuation and not an expert report.
  • A working list of papers a sale in Spain often requires: a land-registry extract, an energy certificate, local property tax and, for a flat, the community papers that apply. I point to them. I do not issue them, and the list is not closed.
  • A description and a [Photo].
  • Arranging viewings.
  • Support through the negotiation and on the way to the deed, alongside the notary and the professionals you choose.

Letting a home

For someone who wants to let. You decide the terms; I keep the practical steps in order.

  • The terms you want to set: length, [Rent], deposit and use.
  • Presentation of the property and a [Photo].
  • Receiving applications and comparing them with criteria we have agreed in writing.
  • The contract is prepared or reviewed by the professional you choose. I do not sign in your place, and I do not replace a lawyer or a gestoría.

Buying or renting

For someone looking for a home. This website does not yet have a real portfolio: if there is nothing to show, I will say so.

  • A search profile: area, type of home, [Budget] and timing.
  • A shortlist only when real properties fit.
  • Viewings, with time to see the condition of the property and to ask questions.
  • The later steps in order, with the bank if there is finance, with the notary and with your adviser. The decision to lend is not mine.
