How I work

Five steps, with no shortcuts

The same outline applies whether you are selling, letting or searching. Nothing starts before the written proposal.

  1. Conversation

    You tell me what you want to do, by when, and what concerns you. This first conversation does not oblige you to instruct me.

  2. Viewing or search

    I see the property, or we narrow the area, the kind of home and the conditions that matter.

  3. Written proposal

    I set out what I will do, what sits outside the work, and the fees: [Fees – not yet set]. I do not continue without your agreement.

  4. Getting started

    Wording, a [Photo] and viewings, as agreed. This website has no real properties to show today.

  5. Through to signature

    I stay with you for the negotiation and for coordination with the notary’s office and, if needed, with your lawyer or gestoría.

Where I work

Neighbourhoods in Las Palmas

For now I concentrate on these neighbourhoods in the city. This is not a ranking of the best areas and not a price forecast. It is the map on which I can give you close attention.

  • Las Canteras / Guanarteme

    Facing Playa de Las Canteras, in the north of the city. A promenade, neighbourhood shops and residential buildings.

  • Santa Catalina

    Around Parque de Santa Catalina and the port. An urban grid, with hotels, shops and the way through to the beach.

  • Ciudad Jardín

    A residential neighbourhood of quiet streets, between the port and the centre.

  • Alcaravaneras

    Beside Playa de Las Alcaravaneras and the marina, south of Santa Catalina.

  • Triana

    The historic shopping axis, around Calle Mayor de Triana.

  • Vegueta

    The founding quarter of the city, with pedestrian streets and the cathedral of Santa Ana.

If your matter sits outside this list, tell me. I will say plainly whether I can take it on.
